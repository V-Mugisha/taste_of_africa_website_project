North Africa

Fragrant, spiced, and slow-cooked.

Food Culture

North African cuisine blends Mediterranean, Arab, and Berber traditions, defined by fragrant spices, olives, and slow-cooked dishes that bring families together around a shared table.

Staples

  • Couscous
  • Wheat bread
  • Olives and dates
  • Lamb
  • Chickpeas
  • Preserved lemons

Cooking Techniques

Dishes are defined by slow braising in clay tagines and by complex spice blends such as ras el hanout, balanced with the bright tang of preserved lemons.

Signature Dishes

  • Moroccan Tagine
  • Couscous
  • Harira soup
