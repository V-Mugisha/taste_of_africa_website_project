East Africa

Fermented, stewed, and coastally spiced.

Food Culture

East African cuisine ranges from Ethiopia's fermented injera to the coconut-rich curries of the Swahili coast, shaped by centuries of Indian Ocean trade.

Staples

  • Teff
  • Maize and beans
  • Cassava leaves
  • Plantains
  • Coconut
  • Cloves

Cooking Techniques

Ethiopian cooks master fermentation for injera, while coastal kitchens simmer stews in coconut milk perfumed with cloves and cardamom. Slow stewing is the heart of the region's cooking.

Signature Dishes

  • Injera
  • Isombe
  • Ugali
