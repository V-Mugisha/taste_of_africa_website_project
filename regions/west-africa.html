West Africa

Bold, smoky, and deeply spiced.

Food Culture

West African cuisine is bold, communal, and deeply spiced. Built around grains, tubers, and fiery chilies, meals are often shared straight from a single pot, and food is central to every celebration.

Staples

  • Rice
  • Yams and cassava
  • Plantains
  • Millet
  • Groundnuts
  • Okra
  • Palm oil

Cooking Techniques

Cooks rely on pounding and grinding to unlock flavour, slow one-pot simmering to build depth, and palm oil to give dishes their signature red-gold colour. Fish and meat are often smoked for a deep, savoury richness.

Signature Dishes

  • Jollof Rice
  • Fufu and soup
  • Groundnut stew
