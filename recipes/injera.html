Injera

East Africa

About This Dish

Injera is a spongy, sour flatbread made from fermented teff flour. It serves as both plate and utensil in Ethiopian cuisine, scooping up richly spiced stews known as wot.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups teff flour
  • 3 cups water
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon baking soda (optional)

Instructions

Show step-by-step instructions
  1. Mix the teff flour with water to form a smooth batter.
  2. Cover and let it ferment at room temperature for 2 to 3 days.
  3. Stir in the salt and baking soda just before cooking.
  4. Heat a flat pan and pour a thin layer of batter in a spiral.
  5. Cover and cook until the surface is covered in holes and the edges lift.
  6. Remove and stack the injera under a cloth to keep it soft.
