Isombe

East Africa

About This Dish

Isombe is a hearty stew of pounded cassava leaves simmered with groundnuts. Popular in Rwanda, Burundi, and the DRC, it is rich, earthy, and deeply comforting.

Ingredients

  • 500 g cassava leaves, pounded
  • 1 cup groundnut (peanut) paste
  • 2 onions, chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 tablespoons palm oil
  • 1 eggplant, diced (optional)
  • Salt to taste

Instructions

Show step-by-step instructions
  1. Heat the oil and sauté the onions and garlic until soft.
  2. Add the pounded cassava leaves and stir well.
  3. Mix the groundnut paste with water and pour it in.
  4. Add the eggplant if using, and season with salt.
  5. Simmer gently, stirring, until thick and cooked through.
  6. Serve with rice, plantains, or ugali.
