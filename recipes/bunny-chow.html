Bunny Chow

Southern Africa

About This Dish

Bunny chow is a Durban street-food icon: a hollowed-out loaf of bread filled with spicy curry. Born in the city's Indian community, it is traditionally eaten with the hands.

Ingredients

  • 1 loaf of white bread
  • 500 g beef or chicken, cubed
  • 2 onions, diced
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 2 tablespoons curry powder
  • 1 teaspoon turmeric
  • 1 teaspoon garam masala
  • 2 potatoes, cubed
  • 2 tablespoons oil
  • Salt to taste

Instructions

Show step-by-step instructions
  1. Heat the oil and fry the onions until golden.
  2. Add the curry powder, turmeric, and garam masala; cook for a minute.
  3. Add the meat and brown on all sides.
  4. Add the tomatoes and potatoes with a little water.
  5. Simmer until the meat is tender and the curry thickens.
  6. Cut the loaf into quarters and hollow out the centre.
  7. Fill each hollow with hot curry and serve.
