Moroccan Tagine

North Africa

About This Dish

A tagine is both a conical clay cooking vessel and the slow-cooked stew made in it. Fragrant with spices, dried fruit, and tender meat, it is a cornerstone of Moroccan hospitality.

Ingredients

  • 500 g chicken or lamb
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 teaspoon ground ginger
  • 1 teaspoon cumin
  • 1 teaspoon cinnamon
  • A pinch of saffron
  • 1/2 cup dried apricots
  • 1/2 cup green olives
  • 1 preserved lemon
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions

Show step-by-step instructions
  1. Heat the olive oil in the tagine base and brown the meat on all sides.
  2. Add the onion and garlic, cooking until soft.
  3. Stir in the spices and cook until fragrant.
  4. Add enough water to cover, then simmer gently.
  5. Add the dried apricots, olives, and preserved lemon.
  6. Cover and cook on low heat until the meat is tender.
  7. Serve with warm bread or couscous.
