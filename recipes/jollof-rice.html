Jollof Rice

West Africa

About This Dish

Jollof rice is a beloved West African one-pot dish of rice simmered in a rich tomato and pepper sauce. A source of friendly rivalry between Nigeria and Ghana, it is the centrepiece of parties and celebrations across the region.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups long-grain parboiled rice
  • 4 large tomatoes
  • 2 red bell peppers
  • 1 scotch bonnet pepper
  • 1 onion
  • 1/2 cup tomato paste
  • 1/2 cup vegetable oil
  • 2 teaspoons curry powder
  • 1 teaspoon thyme
  • 2 cups chicken or vegetable stock
  • Salt and seasoning to taste

Instructions

Show step-by-step instructions
  1. Blend the tomatoes, bell peppers, scotch bonnet, and half the onion into a smooth purée.
  2. Heat the oil in a pot and fry the remaining diced onion until golden.
  3. Add the tomato paste and fry for 2 to 3 minutes.
  4. Pour in the pepper purée and cook until the sauce thickens and the oil separates.
  5. Add the curry powder, thyme, and seasoning; stir well.
  6. Pour in the stock and bring to a boil.
  7. Add the washed rice, reduce the heat, cover, and simmer until the rice is tender.
  8. Fluff with a fork and serve hot.
