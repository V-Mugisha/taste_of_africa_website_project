About Us

Celebrating the heritage, stories, and flavours of African cuisine.

Our Mission

We are on a mission to promote African food heritage, celebrating the continent's diverse cuisines, sharing authentic recipes, and telling the stories behind every dish. We believe food is one of the most powerful ways to understand a culture and bring people together.

History of African Cuisine

African cuisine is among the oldest and most influential in the world, rooted in ancient grains such as sorghum, millet, and teff. Over centuries, trade, migration, and agriculture have shaped a rich patchwork of regional traditions, from the spice routes of North Africa to the Swahili coast's blend of African, Arab, and Indian flavours.

Global Influence

Africa's culinary influence reaches far beyond the continent. The Atlantic slave trade carried okra, black-eyed peas, and yams to the Americas, giving rise to dishes such as gumbo and Hoppin' John, while ingredients like coffee and watermelon trace their roots to African soil. Today, chefs around the world are rediscovering these flavours with fresh appreciation.
